## Findings

### L-1 · **High** · The kill switch and a bracket leg raced → the account ended **SHORT 1, unprotected**
**Seen (S5, MotiveWave log 04:04:32 IST):** long 1 from 4298.5, bracket stop 4297.8. `.875` the **stop leg filled**
(SIM-166, SELL STP 4297.8) — closing the long, and that loss took the session past −10 ticks. On the same price
move the pipeline's kill switch fired; the runtime read the account position as **still 1**
(`KILL_SWITCH_TRIGGERED … "positionBefore":1`), so it sent **close at market** (SIM-168, SELL MKT, filled 4297.8)
and then cancelled all orders (incl. the target leg). Two sells against one long → **SHORT 1, no stop, no target**,
study disarmed. The user closed it by hand at 04:05:46 (BUY 4297.5). **The double-fill check (D-87) did not catch
it:** the kill switch had already made the order tracker forget its legs, so both fills were journaled as
`untracked` and no mismatch check ran.
**Cause:** `plumbingEdgeCases.md` §7 / §10 seen live — the kill switch (drain thread) reads `ctx.getPosition()`
before the platform has applied a leg fill that is being delivered on another thread (see L-2). Code review A1's
"never close a flat account" guard could not help: the account *looked* long. The P&L the kill switch acts on is the
risk chain's estimate (C1), which crossed −10 on the same tick.

### L-2 · **High** · `OrderContext.getPosition()` lags — it did not show a manual close for two minutes
**Seen (S5):** the user's manual close filled at **04:05:46** (MotiveWave `closePosition`); at **04:07:47** the
control-box deactivation logged `DEACTIVATE pos=-1` — still the short. One second later the re-activation read
`pos=0`. Together with L-1's `positionBefore:1`, the account position read by the runtime is **not current** at the
moment it matters. Everything that decides from it — `reconcileLive`, the kill switch, the session flatten,
`refuseToArmReason`, the double-fill check — inherits the lag. **Unverified:** whether it lags only for fills the
study did not place, or always by some delay.

### L-3 · **Med** · Fills the study did not place never reach the journal
**Seen:** (a) S3 — the user removed the study with a short open and answered **yes** to MotiveWave's "close the
running position?" dialog; the platform closed it after the study was destroyed; (b) S5 — the manual close at
04:05:46. Neither fill produced an `ORDER_FILLED`/`order_fill`. **Consequence:** the daily report shows those trades
as open/untracked and `status.py` keeps saying `position: SHORT 1 (1 open trade)` while the account is flat. The
journal's idea of the position is only as good as the fills routed to the study.

### L-4 · **Med** · MotiveWave offers "close the running position?" on removal although the study says it does not support close-on-deactivate
**Seen:** S3 removal with a short open → dialog → the platform closed the position. `@StudyHeader
supportsCloseOnDeactivate = false` did not suppress the dialog. A platform-side close outside our code (and outside
the journal, L-3). The runbook should say which answer to give; the user answered "yes" in S3.

### L-5 · **Med** · The strategy's own stop/target view diverges from the real bracket (C1 live evidence)
**Seen:** S2 trade 3 — price went **100 %** of the way to the target (trade viewer: touched 4301.1), the strategy
emitted `target_hit`, but the **limit order did not fill**; price reversed and the **stop** filled (−0.6 pts). The
risk chain booked a win; the account lost. S5 — the strategy called its own exits four times while the real short
stayed open 36 s more. Harmless for execution (exits are bracket-only) but the risk chain's P&L (and so the kill
switch, L-1) is computed from these virtual exits, not the fills.

### L-6 · **Med** · Brackets are placed from the signal price, not the fill (code review B8, seen live)
**Seen:** S2 trade 2 — market entry slipped **4 ticks** at the open; target 1 tick from the fill, stop 9 ticks.

### L-7 · **Low** · The platform now cancels the sibling bracket leg itself
**Seen:** every leg fill is preceded by `ORDER_CANCELLED <sibling>` and `POSITION_CLOSED pos=0`, **before** our
fill callback — so our explicit sibling cancel finds nothing to do (no `LIVE_SIBLING_LEG_CANCELLED` lines).
Contradicts the 2026-09-21 observation "no OCO". Harmless; our code handles an already-cancelled sibling.

### L-8 · **Low** · `SEVERE OrderImpl::target order not found!` in MotiveWave's log on every bracket
**Seen:** after each entry fill and each leg fill. MotiveWave-internal (probably the same linkage as L-7); no
visible effect so far. Recorded so it isn't rediscovered as a new problem.

### L-9 · **Low** · Stop legs fill 1–2 ticks worse than their trigger
**Seen:** S2 trade 1 stop 4302.9 → filled 4302.7; trade 3 stop 4302.1 → 4302.2. Stop-market slippage on a fast open;
expected, but it makes the 5-tick bracket asymmetric in practice (with L-6).

### L-10 · **Low** · Misleading journal text after deactivation
**Seen:** S3 after `DEACTIVATE`: `reconcile_skipped … "OrderGateway not yet constructed (onActivate has not
fired)"` — the gateway had been torn down by the deactivation, not "not yet constructed". Nothing was sent (correct).

### L-11 · **Low** · Strategy signal chatter on a thin early profile
**Seen:** `lvn_fade_test` flipping between "entered from above/below" several times a second on 1-tick LVN zones in
the first minutes (S2). Contained by churn/rate limits; expected for a stress-test strategy on a young profile.

### L-12 · **Low** · Friday's last bar arrives as the first live bar close
**Seen:** 17:00:00 CT, `onBarClose` delivered the bar that was still forming when the market shut on Friday (end
16:00 CT Friday) — the warm-start had deliberately skipped it as "forming". Recorded under Friday's day file
(`bars/GC/20720.jsonl`, `market_structure/GC/20720.jsonl`). Correct by its timestamp; surprising to find.

### L-13 · **Low** · Two `warm_start` lines in one day's market-structure file
**Seen:** S1 (1-min chart) and S2 (20 s) are both `@GC` on the same trading day, so `market_structure/GC/20722.jsonl`
holds a 1-min warm-start followed by the 20 s one. The later line is the one that counts; readers must use the last.

### L-14 · **High** · The kill switch fired late — it acts on the risk chain's estimated P&L, not the account's
**Seen (S5, limit 10 ticks):** the real account's realized P&L for the session reached **−$100 = −10 ticks at
22:33:17 UTC** (the stop fill after the 2nd losing trade: `sdkTotalRealizedPnL −100.0`). The kill switch did **not**
fire then; trading continued (a winner, then a loser: −12 ticks at 22:34:18) and it fired only at **~22:34:27–32**, on
a price move, when the account was at **−19 ticks** after L-1. **Cause:** `RiskChain` computes the daily loss from
the strategy's accepted intents at the tick price (entry at signal price, exits at the strategy's *virtual*
stop/target — L-5), not from fills — code review B7/C1 seen live. With a 200-tick limit the same gap exists, just
less visible. **Not fixed.**

### L-15 · **Low** · `sdkTotalRealizedPnL` is per study activation, not account or day
**Seen:** reset to 0 at every activation (S5 started at 0.0 with the account ~−$1,000 down). The report's per-trade
numbers are unaffected (they use prices and `cash Δ`), but anything that sums "today's realized P&L" must not read
this field across sessions. Use the cash balance for the account.

### L-16 · **Low** · Two `ORDER_MODIFIED` callbacks on every bracket submission
**Seen:** each `LIVE_BRACKET_SUBMITTED` is preceded by `ORDER_MODIFIED <stop>` and `ORDER_MODIFIED <target>`
(platform status updates on submit). Logged only; no handling needed so far. Recorded so it isn't mistaken for an
anomaly.

### L-17 · **Low** · Bracket prices journaled as float expansions
**Seen:** `"stopPrice":4302.89990234375` for 4302.9 — `OrderGateway.submitRealBracket` journals the `float` sent to
the SDK as a double. The price sent is on the grid (A9); only the journal text is ugly. The report already rounds it
(shows 4302.9). Cosmetic.

### L-18 · **Low** · `order_fill.t` (local) and `lastFillTimeMs` (exchange) differ by ~0.2–1 s
**Seen:** e.g. S5 3rd entry: `t` 22:31:26, fill time 22:31:27. Two clocks, as designed (the report uses
`lastFillTimeMs` for trade times). Worth knowing when lining fills up with the 1-second candles (which use the local
clock).
