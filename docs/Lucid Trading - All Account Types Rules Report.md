# Lucid Trading - All Account Types Rules Report

Nov 4, 2026 · @yoda

## Comparison of all account types

Lucid sells four public plans (Pro, Flex, Daily, Direct) in four sizes each ($25K, $50K, $100K, $150K), plus an invite-only Maxx program, a legacy Black plan and Live accounts you reach after funded. Figures are from Lucid's pricing cards and help center on 4 Nov 2026; items marked (derived) are my arithmetic. For Pro 25K in depth, see the earlier Pro 25K report.

| Route | Evaluation, then funded | Evaluation, then funded | Evaluation, then funded | Straight to funded, no evaluation |
| 25K price (list / with coupon) | $108 / $75.60 | $65 / $45.50 | $86 / $60.20 | $329 / $230.30 |
| 25K reset fee | $75 | $45 | $60 | Not stated |
| 25K profit target | $1,250 | $1,250 | $1,250 | None |

| Funded consistency | 40% | None | None | 20% |
| Daily loss limit | Optional, soft | Optional, soft | Optional, soft | 50K and up only; none on 25K |
| Payout gate (25K) | $250 cycle profit, 40% consistency, balance above $26,100 buffer | 5 days at $100+ profit, positive net profit, no buffer | Balance above $26,100 buffer, positive net profit | $1,500 cycle profit first ($1,250 later), 20% consistency |

Maxx is invite-only and Black is legacy; both have their own sections below. All four public plans cap size at 2 minis or 20 micros on the 25K.

## LucidFlex

Flex is the cheapest evaluation, has no consistency rule and no buffer once funded, but payouts are capped at 50% of profit and an unusual MLL lock-in applies when you request one.

| Size | Profit target | MLL | Max size | Fee list / coupon | Reset | DLL (optional) | Payout cap | Min daily profit for a counted day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 25K | $1,250 | $1,000 | 2 minis / 20 micros | $65 / $45.50 | $45 | $600 | 50% of profit, max $1,000 | $100 |
| 50K | $3,000 | $2,000 | 4 / 40 | $115 / $80.50 | $80 | $1,200 | max $2,000 | $150 |
| 100K | $6,000 | $3,000 | 6 / 60 | $215 / $150.50 | $150 | $1,800 | max $2,500 | $200 |
| 150K | $9,000 | $4,500 | 10 / 100 | $323 / $226.10 | $225 | $2,700 | max $3,000 | $250 |

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Customization | Chosen at purchase and locked: DLL on or off, and consistency 40% or 50%. Four configurations |
| DLL | Soft breach: locked out until next session. DLL ON lowers the price and enables On Demand Payouts; OFF costs more and pays at end of day |
| Evaluation consistency | Largest day at most 40% (50% with the add-on) of evaluation profit. Fastest pass is 3 days at 40%, 2 days at 50% |
| Evaluation limits | No time limit, no activation fee, upgrade 5 to 30 minutes after the target |
| Funded consistency | None |
| Funded buffer | None |
| Drawdown | EOD trailing like Pro: Initial Trail Balance $26,100, locked MLL balance $25,100 on the 25K |
| MLL on payout | When you request a payout, the MLL automatically moves to the locked MLL balance ($25,100 on the 25K) |
| Payout gate | Five separate days in the cycle at or above the minimum daily profit, plus positive net profit in the cycle. Both reset after each approved payout |
| Payout size | $500 minimum; maximum is 50% of profit up to the cap in the table; caps do not rise with later payouts |
| Payout count | Up to 5 per account, then the account moves toward live |
| Split and timing | 90/10; no fixed window; paid within 2 business days |
| On Demand Payouts | DLL ON only; request mid-session from that session's profit; trading then pauses until the end of the day; unavailable after a hedging violation |
| Scaling plan (funded only) | 25K: $0 to $999 profit trades 1 mini or 10 micros; $1,000 and up trades 2 minis or 20 micros. Updates at session end and falls if a payout lowers your profit. 50K starts at 2 minis and reaches its 4-mini cap at $2,000 profit; 100K starts at 3 and reaches 6 at $3,000; 150K starts at 4 and reaches 10 at $4,500 |

Derived: a $500 payout needs at least $1,000 of profit because the cap is 50% of profit. Because the MLL jumps to $25,100 at the moment you request a payout, a balance only slightly above that leaves almost no room.

## LucidDaily

Daily pays out every day with no per-request cap and no funded consistency rule, but funded accounts always use an intraday drawdown, U.S. red-folder news is a hard breach, and hitting a profit ceiling pushes you live.

| 50K | $3,000 | $2,000 | 4 / 40 | $122 / $85.40 | $85 | $1,200 | $8,000 | $52,100 |
| 100K | $6,000 | $3,000 | 6 / 60 | $200 / $140 | $140 | $1,800 | $10,000 | $103,100 |
| 150K | $9,000 | $4,500 | 10 / 100 | $286 / $200.20 | $200 | $2,700 | $12,000 | $154,600 |

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Customization | Chosen at purchase and locked: DLL on or off; evaluation drawdown EOD or intraday; consistency 40% or 50%. Eight configurations. Intraday evaluation is cheaper than EOD |
| Evaluation drawdown | EOD ignores unrealized P&L and trails session-end balance. Intraday includes unrealized P&L and trails in real time |
| Funded drawdown | Always intraday, whatever you picked for the evaluation. Same trail and lock figures as Pro: initial trail $26,100, locked at $25,100 on the 25K |
| Evaluation consistency | Largest day at most 50% of profit per the evaluation and consistency articles; the pricing card offers 40% or 50%. Built-in cushion allows a two-day pass |
| DLL | Soft breach, fixed for the session and does not trail. If the MLL trails above the DLL, hitting the MLL is a hard breach |
| Funded consistency | None |
| High-impact news | Funded: any U.S. red-folder (impact High, currency USD) news is a hard breach and breaches the account. You must be flat from 1 minute before to 1 minute after the event |
| Payout gate | Balance above the buffer and positive net profit since the last payout |
| Payout size | $500 minimum; maximum is simply the profit above the buffer; no per-request cap |
| Daily payouts | Allowed on any day you qualify; with DLL ON you can request mid-session after the dashboard shows objectives complete, and more than once per session |
| Split and timing | 90/10; paid within 2 business days |
| Live | Earning the max daily profit moves you live automatically; risk team can also move you for large lifetime payouts or exceptional performance |
| Live transition | Buffer balance funds the starting live drawdown and cannot be withdrawn; sim profit above the buffer is payable only after broker KYC, capped at $15,000 in total; moved live each evening after the 6 PM EST report; all sim accounts then close |

## LucidLive

Live is a real-capital futures account that Lucid's risk team grants at its discretion after funded success; it starts at $0 with a tight drawdown and replaces your simulated accounts.

| Funded size | Starting live drawdown | Max live size | Live profit target | Live bonus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 25K | $1,000 | 2 minis / 20 micros | $2,000 | $1,000 |
| 50K | $2,000 | 4 / 40 | $4,000 | $2,000 |
| 100K | $3,000 | 6 / 60 | $6,000 | $3,000 |
| 150K | $4,500 | 10 / 100 | $9,000 | $4,500 |

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Who enters the review pool | Pro, Flex, Direct: final payout (payout 5), large lifetime payouts, exceptional sim performance, or earlier live status. Daily: reaching the max daily profit, large lifetime payouts, exceptional performance, or earlier live status. Always at the risk team's discretion |
| Eligibility | One live account per funded account, and each needs at least one payout. All eligible accounts move live together |
| Account terms | $0 starting balance, daily payouts, EOD drawdown, no daily loss limit, no consistency rule |
| MLL lock | MLL trails EOD highs, then locks at $100 once live profit equals the starting drawdown; requesting a live payout before that also locks it at $100 |
| Live bonus | Reach the live profit target and complete 20 trading days (a day is one or more trades with net P&L of at least +$1 or -$1 after fees); repeatable per account; 90/10; "contract flipping" invites review |
| Cashout alternative | 50% of the live starting balance, 90/10; you get 24 hours after the move-to-live email or the move happens automatically |
| Cooldown | 2 weeks before buying a new evaluation after blowing a live account; longer for repeated reckless blowups |
| Sim accounts close | All simulated accounts close when you go live or cash out. An ineligible funded account that does not move live is refunded only if at least 50% of its drawdown remains (Pro article) |
| Household | If one household member trades live, the others may not trade sim accounts |
| Live scaling (25K) | $0 to $1,999.99 profit: 1 mini or 10 micros (COMEX metals: 0 minis or 5 micros). $2,000 and up: 2 minis or 20 micros (COMEX: 1 mini or 10 micros). Updates at end of day; volatile contracts may be reduced; attempts to circumvent are tracked |
| Hedging | Banned by Lucid and the CME on live accounts, including via copiers; violation is a permanent ban |
| Legacy Pro (bought or reset by 27 Feb 2026) | Live after the sixth payout; sim profit carried over capped at $15,000 / $20,000 / $25,000 / $30,000 by size, excess forfeited; Day 1 deposit up to $2,000 / $3,000 / $4,000 / $5,000; rest in escrow released at $5,000 per $10,000 live profit after 10 profitable days and $10,000 profit; withdrawals only after 60 days |
| Legacy Flex (bought or reset by 27 Feb 2026) | Live after the sixth payout; caps $4,000 / $8,000 / $12,000 / $16,000; Day 1 deposit $1,200 / $2,400 / $3,600 / $4,800; same escrow mechanics; an older flat $5,000 cap applies to earlier onboarding |
| Daily live cap | Sim profit above the buffer payable only after broker KYC, capped at $15,000 in total |

## Hidden and conflicting rules

The most serious ones are Flex's MLL lock at payout request, Daily's hard news breach and permanent intraday drawdown, and Direct's 20% consistency.

| Plan | Rule | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flex | When you request a payout the MLL moves to the locked balance ($25,100 on the 25K), and there is no buffer | A payout taken with a balance near $25,100 leaves almost no room and can breach the account on the next trade |
| Flex | Payout max is 50% of profit but one page says "account balance"; caps never rise | Confirm the basis before sizing expectations |
| Flex | Scaling plan cuts contracts when a payout lowers profit | After a payout you may drop from 2 minis to 1 on the 25K |
| Flex | Consistency applies to the evaluation only; add-on 50% costs extra | Funded profit days are the real gate: five days at the minimum |
| Daily | Funded drawdown is always intraday even if you bought EOD for the evaluation | Open profit raises the trail; a pullback from an intraday peak can breach |
| Daily | USD red-folder news is a hard breach on funded; the evaluation wording is not stated | Calendar discipline is mandatory; flat 1 minute either side |
| Daily | Reaching the max daily profit ($6,000 on the 25K) moves you live automatically | A big day ends the sim account and closes your other sim accounts |
| Daily | Live carry-over capped at $15,000 total and buffer stays locked in the account | Sim profit above that is not payable |
| Daily | Evaluation article says 50% consistency; customization and card say 40% or 50% | Verify the option you buy |
| Direct | 20% consistency means at least five profit days; goals are $1,500 then $1,250 on the 25K | Slowest, most expensive route per dollar of payout; patience required |
| Direct | 25K has no DLL and no On Demand Payouts; 100K and 150K MLL ($3,500 / $5,000) differ from the other plans | Do not assume Pro or Flex figures for Direct |
| Direct | "No caps on simulated payouts" yet each payout is capped | Treat the table as binding |
| Pro | Eval and DLL articles list the 25K DLL as none; Daily's DLL article and the pricing card list $600 | Likely stale pages; confirm what you are buying |
| All | Payout 5 for current accounts; legacy accounts (bought or reset by 27 Feb 2026) get a sixth payout; other cutoffs 19 Jan 2026 and 28 Nov 2025 | Check your purchase date against each article |
| All | On Demand session is stated as 6:00 PM to 3:45 PM ET but flatten time is 4:45 PM ET | One of the two is likely a typo |
| All | Dashboard lags 5 to 30 minutes | Keep your own MLL and consistency tracker |
| All | Request is final; trading after a request can deny it | Stop trading once submitted |
| Maxx | Invite only; "full porting near the live MLL" can mean extended cooldowns or permanent removal | It rewards steady sizing, not all-in risk |
| Maxx | New program price not published; old tiers rose with blown live accounts and ended 4 Oct 2026 | Price is unknown until you are invited |
| Live | All eligible accounts move live together and sim accounts close; household cannot keep trading sim if one member is live | Plan multi-person households carefully |
| Terms | Last modified 20 March 2025, effective immediately on change, enforcement at Lucid's sole discretion | Re-read before each purchase |

## Which account fits which strategy

Pick by how your profits arrive and what you trade around, not by price. This is a rules comparison, not advice on what to buy.

| If your strategy... | Best fit | Watch out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Has lumpy profits with a few big days | Flex funded or Daily funded (no funded consistency) | Pro needs 40%, Direct needs 20% every payout cycle |
| Trades through U.S. red-folder news | Pro, Flex or Direct | Daily funded makes it a hard breach |
| Wants the lowest entry cost | Flex (25K at $45.50 with coupon) | MLL lock at payout request, 50%-of-profit payout cap |
| Wants daily cash-outs without a per-payout cap | Daily | Intraday drawdown in funded; max daily profit forces you live |
| Wants to skip the evaluation | Direct | Highest fee; $1,500 first cycle goal; 5 profit days minimum on the 25K |
| Wants the biggest early payout on a 25K | Pro ($1,000 then $1,500) | Needs balance above the $26,100 buffer |
| Runs holding times under 5 seconds or very high order rates | None | Microscalping and HFT are banned on every plan |
| Hedges across accounts or copies opposing trades | None | Banned on every plan, including correlated markets |

Open questions to put to Lucid support in writing:

- Which DLL and consistency options are actually sold on each plan today, since pages disagree (Pro 25K DLL, Daily evaluation consistency)?
- Does Flex's MLL lock at payout request apply the moment you submit, and does a balance below $25,100 breach immediately?
- Is Daily's USD news ban also a hard breach on the evaluation?
- Does exactly 20% (Direct) or 40% (Pro) count as meeting the rule?
- Is the On Demand session 3:45 PM or 4:45 PM ET, and does EST follow daylight time?
- What is the price and benchmark for the new Maxx program?
- Is a non-US trader allowed a US-hosted VPS for automation?
