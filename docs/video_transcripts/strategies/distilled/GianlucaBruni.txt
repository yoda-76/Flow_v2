PART B. MEASURABLE PRIMITIVES
======================================================================
B1. VALID CANDLE: body size > the candle's longest wick (upper or lower, whichever is bigger), in ticks. [Q][S][01:12:44]
    Whiteboard: body 4 / wick 3 -> valid; body 3 / wick 6 -> not valid. Chart: body 16 / wick 56 -> not valid;
    body 54 / wick 24 -> valid. [01:20:41, 01:21:12]
B2. ENGULFING (his definition — NOT the textbook bullish engulfing): the candle's body CLOSES beyond the previous
    candle's high (long) / low (short), i.e. beyond the previous candle's wick extreme. Candle size does not matter.
    Close must be beyond the previous high by >= the threshold (B4). [Q][01:06:51-01:08:06, 01:24:45, 01:26:14]
    [S] Previous candle need NOT be opposite colour: whiteboard shows blue engulfing blue; chart re-entries engulf
        green candles.
B3. VAH BREAK: candle closes above the previous session's VAH (long) by >= the threshold (B4). Smaller close-through
    = not broken; wait for another candle. [Q][S][01:17:52-01:19:00] Whiteboard: 0.6 beyond the line = broken,
    0.4 = not.
B4. THRESHOLD for B2/B3: 0.6 for forex, 6 for NQ. [Q][01:17:44, 01:24:53]
    [?] Unit. TradingView's ruler shows "points (pct) ticks" (e.g. his stop label "28.25 (0.095%) 113"), and he reads
        the first number — so 6 on NQ is most likely 6 POINTS (= 24 ticks). For gold there is no value: must be set by
        backtest (rough NQ-equivalent by volatility would be a few dollars on GC, but that is a guess).
B5. PRICE EFFICIENCY ("low effort, big result") — the volume condition on the trigger candle. [Q][S]
    (a) REDUCING VOLUME: trigger candle's volume < previous candle's volume (falling while price engulfs), even if
        still above the MA. [01:12:58, 01:19:12]
    (b) LOW EFFORT: trigger candle's volume < volume MA(20), even if rising vs the previous candle. [01:26:50, 01:27:17]
    Either (a) or (b) satisfies the entry. Both = "combination". Fails if volume is rising AND above the MA
    ("high effort, high result") -> no trade. [01:28:00, 01:29:00]
    [S] Whiteboard (00:54:23): two red candles, then two blue candles each closing above the previous high; volume
        bars red-red above the MA, then blue-blue smaller and falling to the MA line. The rule as spoken is
        one-bar (trigger vs previous, 01:19:12); the sketch is consistent with that.
B6. MARKET STRUCTURE LOW (longs; mirror = MS HIGH for shorts). [Q][S][01:12:10-01:16:01, 01:20:16-01:22:40]
    - Take the most recent pullback before the breakout (break of structure).
    - Walk back from the breakout; take the CLOSEST valid (B1) bearish candle in that pullback.
    - None in this pullback -> go back to the previous pullback and repeat.
    - MS low = the LOWEST LOW of that pullback ("we don't check if this candle is valid, we take the lowest level").
      [S] On the NQ chart the MS-low line sits at the swing low of the pullback (~29,640), not at the valid candle.
    - A low not anchored by a valid candle "means nothing" when broken. [01:15:22]
    [D] "Breakout / break of structure" itself is not defined (presumably: close above the prior swing high).
B7. MARKET STRUCTURE HIGH = TARGET CEILING for longs. [S] On the whiteboard it's the peak marked "K"; on the NQ chart a
    line at the prior swing high (~29,966) — the high made BEFORE the pullback into the previous value area.
    [01:04:00, 01:27:28]

PART H. VALIDATION PROTOCOL (how he decides the edge is real)
======================================================================
H1. In-sample / out-of-sample: build on one period (e.g. 2024-25), test unchanged on unseen data (e.g. 2026).
    NEVER change rules during the OOS test. [00:14:03-00:15:08, 00:19:47-00:20:12]
H2. Judge on expectancy (R per 1R risked) and Sharpe, not win rate. [00:23:05-00:24:30]
    (His example "0.2 expectancy, risk $1,000 -> $1,200" is misspoken; 0.2R = +$200 per $1,000 risked.)
H3. Degradation IS -> OOS (and live vs backtest): ~20-25% drift normal; >=30% drop = warning -> lower risk, run a
    failure test; >=50% drop = edge not solid. [00:15:08-00:15:25, 00:20:56-00:22:13]
    [S] His software draws exactly these lines: "warning" = half the record (-50%), "extreme positive" = +50%.
H4. Sharpe: >=1.5 good, ~1.4 still good, <1 not good. [00:17:22]
H5. Overfitting: count the variants you tested; prefer few rules. Chunk test: ~10 parts, check stability. [00:17:35-00:18:52]
H6. Sample size: luck explains 50-100 trades, not 10,000. [00:10:50]
H7. Monitor rolling-60-trade Sharpe and expectancy vs the whole record. [01:30:02-01:30:34]
H8. Monte Carlo: 10,000 shuffles of trade order. [01:37:43]
H9. Include costs (slippage, commission, swap). [01:29:51] [S] His report runs with cost=0.027 (R per trade, it seems).
