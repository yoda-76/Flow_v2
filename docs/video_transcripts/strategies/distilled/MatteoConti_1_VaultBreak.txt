PART B. LEVELS (computed once per session at 00:00 CT, then STATIC for the day)
======================================================================
B1. SESSION = 00:00 CT to 16:00 CT. [I] "from midnight to the end of the trading day ... 4:00 p.m. Central" [00:18:07].
    Midnight anchor (not the 17:00 CT Globex open) is intentional — used for the open, the ATR and the VWAP. [Q]
B2. SESSION OPEN PRICE = OPEN of the 00:00 CT bar. [Q] "what is the price of NQ at midnight central time"; bar choice [U].
B3. DAILY TRUE RANGE (per session, B1) = max(sessHigh, prevClose) − min(sessLow, prevClose),
    where prevClose = the previous session's 16:00 CT close (so the evening gap is included). [U]
    [T] Alternative: plain sessHigh − sessLow. Make it a toggle and compare.
B4. VOLATILITY = SMA, length 15, of DAILY TRUE RANGE (B3), over the 15 COMPLETED sessions before today. [U]
    Basis: "past 15 days ... average true range of all these sessions" [00:12:31]; "the average ATR itself" [00:12:45];
    "a 15 period moving average of ATR ... Not an ATR with a look back of 15" [00:44:30–00:44:37].
    SMA, not EMA — he never mentions exponential smoothing. [U]
    [T] Alternative: SMA15 of ATR(14) (smoother, slower). This is the most sensitive parameter — it sizes the barrier.
B5. NOISE-UP BARRIER = SESSION OPEN PRICE + 0.30 × VOLATILITY. [Q] Fixed for the whole day ("we mark it at midnight").
    No lower barrier (long only). 0.30 was optimized in-sample over 3+ years and held out-of-sample. [Q]
    Note: the published noise-area method varies by time of day; his version is one static level. Code as stated.
B6. VWAP = session VWAP anchored at 00:00 CT ("calculated from midnight as well" [00:54:19]). [Q]

PART C. ENTRY
======================================================================
C1. SIGNAL: a 30m bar CLOSES above NOISE-UP (B5) AND that same bar's close is above VWAP (B6). [Q] Both checked at the
    signal bar's close. [I]
C2. SIGNAL WINDOW: signal bar closes in [10:00, 14:00] CT. [Q][I]
    Window 10:00–14:30 CT for opening positions ("4 and a half hour window — Exactly" [00:16:40]; the "half past 10"
    slip at [00:55:05] corrected to "10 to 14:30, right? — Yes sir"). A signal closing at 10:00 enters at the 10:00
    open. A signal closing at 14:30 would enter at the exit time, so 14:00 is the last useful signal close. [I]
C3. FILL: market order at the OPEN of the next bar. [Q] Always take the fill — no price cap on gap opens. [U]
    Log slippage vs the signal close (gap days are where this rule is expensive). [U]
C4. RE-ENTRY: the condition is a STATE, no fresh cross needed. After an exit (TP or SL), the exit bar itself may be the
    next signal bar if it closes above both NOISE-UP and VWAP; the new trade then fills at the next bar's open. [I]
    ("still above the noise barrier ... still above the VWOP, we trigger another trade" [00:19:30–00:19:57]).
    [T] Alternative: require at least one full bar after the exit.
C5. CAP: max 3 ENTRIES per session ("up to three trades ... no more than that" [00:18:40], [00:19:57]). [Q]
    ONE position at a time — every example is exit-then-new-trade. [I]
C6. No news / event filter ("All the rules have been laid out here"). [Q]
C7. HOLIDAYS / EARLY CLOSES: skip the day entirely (no trade). [U]
    Those days also break the 00:00–16:00 session definition; decide whether they count as one of the 15 sessions in
    B4 when coding (see H3).
