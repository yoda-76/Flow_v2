BASE SPEC (all defaults): NQ, 15m bars, America/Chicago. ONR = 00:00–08:30 CT. Bias from where the 08:30 open sits in
the ONR thirds. OR = the 08:30–08:45 bar. Enter when a bar closes beyond the OR in the bias direction with ADX(14) > 20;

PART C. ENTRY
======================================================================
C1. Long-bias day: a 15m bar CLOSES above the OR high with ADX > 20 on that bar; short-bias day: CLOSES below the OR
    low with ADX > 20. ADX slope irrelevant. Read ADX at the signal bar's close. [Q] [00:41:16–00:41:38]
C2. Fill: market order at the OPEN of the next bar. [Q]
C3. Bias is not invalidated by price action inside the OR bar or by a break of the opposite side first
    [00:46:34–00:46:48]; he rejects extra invalidation conditions [00:46:51–00:47:07]. [Q]
C4. One trade per day. [Q]
C5. EARLIEST ENTRY. Chart example: "we wait like the first one after 9 central time" [01:05:16–01:05:18]; entry
    "not on the candle that broke the opening range high ... because it's after 9:30" [01:05:47–01:05:55]. [Q]
    The first possible signal bar (08:45–09:00) closes at 09:00 and fills at 09:00, so "after 9" is already true.
    6A default: no extra time rule; first fill 09:00 CT. ("9:30" most likely Eastern = 08:30 CT.) [U]
    6B: first fill 09:30 CT.   6C: first fill 09:15 CT.
    Most likely to change the trade count.
C6. FRESH CROSS OR STATE. "breaks above the opening range high and closes above" with ADX > 20 on that candle; the
    interviewer: could be entered at 2 PM "if it stays inside the opening range the whole time" [00:46:24–00:46:34].
    7A default: STATE — any bar after the OR closing beyond it in the bias direction with ADX > 20. [U]
    7B: fresh cross (previous bar closed at or inside the OR).   7C: first break only (if its ADX ≤ 20, no trade that
    day).   7D (low prior): intrabar stop order at the OR boundary.
C7. ADX. No length given; "above 20" only. [Q]
    8A default: Wilder ADX(14) on 15m bars, continuous through the overnight session. [U]
    8B: reset at 08:30.   8C: lengths 10 and 20.   8D: platform built-in ADX (smoothing may differ).
C8. LAST ENTRY. Not stated; interviewer mentions a 2 PM entry [00:46:24].
    9A default: last signal bar closes at 14:15 → last fill 14:15. [U]   9B: last fill 14:00.
    9C: no cutoff except no fill at/after 14:30 (= 9A in practice).   9D: 12:00 / 13:00 (only to see late-trade share).
